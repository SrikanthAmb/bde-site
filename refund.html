Refund Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

We want BDE to be worth what you pay for it. If it isn't working for you, here is what we offer.

Refunds on your first payment

If you are not happy with BDE, ask for a refund within 7 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full.

Renewals

Monthly renewals are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time and BDE will keep working until the end of the month you've paid for. You won't be charged again.

Problems we can't fix

If BDE stops working on your laptop because of a fault on our side that we can't resolve, we'll refund the current month.

How to ask for a refund

Email ambidisrikanth@gmail.com with the email address you used to buy BDE. Refunds are issued by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record, to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

Cancelling

You can cancel from the link in your purchase email from Dodo Payments, or by writing to us.
